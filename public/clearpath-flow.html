Maria is a person, not a bot

The human lane is a real conversation. She sees the user's context, reads the order, and hands back the exact components the product builds. Human warmth, system consistency: one source of truth for both.

The expert door is still guarded

Direct search skips the questions but never the confirmation. Every path converges on the same "both names, three reassurances" screen before money appears. Four entries, one contract.

Three doors by certainty

Order in hand, unsure, or expert: the user self-segments. No upfront quiz, no search bar demanding an answer they don't have.

Guided, never chat

Bounded questions ("1 of 2") with options that teach. Structured triage completes at 90%+; open chat under anxiety stalls. "None of these" is always a valid answer.

Recovery takes the blame

"The gap is on our side." Three self-serve paths plus Maria with full context, and every failed match is logged verbatim as free research.

Words, never numbers

Every figure comes from rate tables and plan status, deterministic by architecture. The model chooses sentences, never prices.

Confidence has a shape

Weaker data widens the range visibly and explains itself. The moment of least certainty gets the most support, never false precision.

The loop no competitor closes

Estimate vs. final bill, reconciled as an experience. One accurate range becomes standing trust for every future estimate. That is the retention story of a twice-a-year product.

Confidence drives depth

Unambiguous input skips clarification entirely. The system asks only when it must. High-confidence matches land straight on this screen.
