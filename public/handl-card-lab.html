Why the current card wastes space

  • The card is a flex row: the 44 px avatar + 13 px gap claim a 57 px column for the card's entire height, but only the top 44 px of it is ever used. That's roughly 57 × 176 px ≈ 15% of the card's inner area permanently blank.
  • Content is squeezed into 237 px instead of the available 294 px — a 24% narrower text column than the card can offer.
  • The label/value meta rows pay for that twice: "Total cost" pushes $3,292 · typical here: $6,200 into a wrap, so the most important number on the screen reads as a cramped afterthought.
  • The CTA is indented under the avatar column, so it doesn't align with anything else on the screen and loses ~20% of its tap width.
  • The card never states the actual win — a $2,908 gap vs. typical — it makes the user subtract.
